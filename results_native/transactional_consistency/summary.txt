Transactional-consistency concurrent-traffic experiment
=========================================================
40,000 G-PDU packets sent at 5,000 pps (8s) via gtpu_blast, concurrently
with 176 atomic tc_bundle modifications (test/toggle_bundle.py) alternating
the tc_bundle_map[0x2000] entry between (far_id=1,qer_id=1) and
(far_id=3,qer_id=3) as fast as the control-plane script could issue them.

Every forwarded packet emits a decision_event (UPF_EMIT_DECISION_EVENTS
build) carrying the (far_id, qer_id, generation, pdr_id) actually used.

Result:
  total events parsed:                 40000
  torn (invalid far/qer pair) events:  0
  generation-parity mismatches:        0
  pair distribution:                   {(3,3): 20190, (1,1): 19810}
  distinct generations observed:       176

Zero packets observed a torn/mixed read across 40,000 packets and 176
concurrent atomic modifications.
